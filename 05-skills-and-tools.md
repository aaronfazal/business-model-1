# 05 — Skills, Tools and Where Jarvis Helps

## Skills to learn, in order

| # | Skill | Why | How to learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One job-management tool, deeply** | It's the heart of the setup | Take free trials of 2–3 tools built for UK trades (e.g. Tradify, Jobber), pick one, and set up a fake carpentry business in it end to end |
| 2 | **Google Business Profile** | Biggest free source of local enquiries | Google's own help guides; practise on the first client |
| 3 | **Sales calls** | Your time should go here | Your charter sales background transfers directly; use the call structure in [04-getting-clients.md](04-getting-clients.md) |
| 4 | **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads** | Needed for the Growth retainer | Meta's free Blueprint courses; start once you have Care clients |
| 5 | **Basic website editing** | Updating client sites | Jarvis builds them; you learn to change text and photos |

Standardise on **one** job-management tool for all clients. Knowing one tool inside out is what makes the 14-day template possible.

## Tools (keeping cost near £0 at the start)

| Need | Tool | Cost to you |
|---|---|---|
| Client job system | Chosen job-management tool | £0 (client pays their subscription) |
| Client websites | Template built in this repo, hosted free (e.g. GitHub Pages or Netlify) | £0; client buys their own domain |
| Your own website / one-pager | Same template | £0 |
| Contracts and proposals | Jarvis drafts; send as PDF | £0 |
| Taking your retainer payments | A UK direct-debit or card payment service (e.g. GoCardless or Stripe) | Small per-payment fee only |
| Your calendar | Google Calendar | £0 |

Keep the £500 budget in reserve: a domain for yourself, business cards for merchant visits, and possibly a basic business insurance policy once you have paying clients.

## Where Jarvis does the heavy lifting
- Building and customising each client website from the template
- Writing quote templates, Google profile descriptions and posts from their intake form
- Drafting the contract, proposal, case studies and monthly reports
- Writing ad copy for Growth clients
- Preparing you for sales calls (role-play objections)
- Keeping this playbook updated as you learn what works
