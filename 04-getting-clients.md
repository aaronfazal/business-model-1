# 04 — Getting Clients

Rule: no paid ads for our own business until retainers cover the cost. The first clients come from people we know and from proof.

## Phase 1 — Founding clients (weeks 3–6): warm network
- [ ] Write down every carpenter, joiner, builder or tradesperson you know, plus people who know them
- [ ] Pick the 2 most likely to say yes and who'll give honest feedback
- [ ] Offer the founding deal (see [01-offer.md](01-offer.md)) in exchange for a testimonial and before/after numbers
- [ ] Deliver using the playbook and **record real hours spent**

**Example first message (adapt to how you normally talk to them):**

> Hi [name], I'm starting a business helping carpentry firms cut their admin: bookings in one place, quotes sent in minutes, invoices chased automatically, and more Google reviews. I'm taking on two businesses at a reduced price in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial. Fancy a 15-minute chat this week to see if it'd help you?

## Phase 2 — Full price (weeks 7–12): proof and local reach
- [ ] Turn each founding client into a one-page case study (before/after numbers + quote from them)
- [ ] Ask every client for introductions; offer £100 per referral who signs
- [ ] Visit local builders' and timber merchants: trades go there daily. Ask if you can leave cards or a flyer
- [ ] Join local trade and community Facebook groups; share useful tips and case studies, not hard sells
- [ ] Look up carpentry firms on Google Maps with few reviews or no website; these have the clearest need. Contact them with a specific observation ("You've got 6 reviews; the two firms above you have 80+")

## Phase 3 — Scale (month 4+)
- [ ] Referral scheme running with every client
- [ ] Short videos showing a client's before/after setup
- [ ] Consider paid ads for yourself once at least £1,000/month is recurring
- [ ] Expand to other trades (plumbers, electricians, decorators) using the same playbook

## The sales call (20–30 min)
1. Ask about how they book, quote and get paid today; let them describe the pain
2. Ask how many hours a week that costs them, and how many quotes they lose to slow replies
3. Show a case study (once you have one)
4. Explain the setup and what's live in 14 days
5. State the price and the retainer options clearly
6. Agree next step: contract + intake form

## Weekly numbers to track
- Conversations started
- Calls booked
- Clients signed
- Monthly recurring income
