# 02 — Delivery Playbook (the template for every client)

Same steps, same order, every client. No custom market research: the client tells us their rates, services and area through the intake form, and everything else is templated.

## Before day 0
- [ ] Contract signed and setup fee paid (or founding deal agreed)
- [ ] Client has completed the intake form ([03-client-intake.md](03-client-intake.md))
- [ ] Logins/access received: Google Business Profile, existing website, Checkatrade (if any), Facebook page

## Day 0 — Kickoff call (45 min)
- [ ] Walk through their intake answers and fill any gaps
- [ ] Agree who on their team books jobs, quotes and invoices
- [ ] Collect 15–30 photos of recent work
- [ ] Book the day-14 handover call before hanging up

## Days 1–4 — Job system
- [ ] Create their account on the chosen job-management tool (client pays the subscription)
- [ ] Add their services and job rates from the intake form
- [ ] Build 3–5 quote templates for their most common jobs
- [ ] Add staff and set up the shared calendar
- [ ] Set up invoice template and automatic payment reminders (e.g. 3, 7 and 14 days overdue)
- [ ] Import existing customers if they have a list
- [ ] Test: book a dummy job → quote → invoice → reminder

## Days 4–7 — Google profile and reviews
- [ ] Complete every section of their Google Business Profile
- [ ] Upload the best 10+ work photos
- [ ] Turn on automatic review requests when a job is marked complete
- [ ] Send a one-off review request to their last 20–50 happy customers
- [ ] Tidy Checkatrade profile to match (if a member)

## Days 7–12 — Website
- [ ] **No site / poor site:** build from the carpenter template (services, photos, reviews, service area, quote form)
- [ ] **Good site already:** add the quote form, link reviews, fix obvious issues
- [ ] Quote form sends enquiries straight into the job system or their inbox
- [ ] Connect their domain and check it works on a phone

## Day 14 — Handover call (30 min)
- [ ] Show the system working: a job going from enquiry → quote → invoice → paid
- [ ] Show early numbers: new reviews, profile views
- [ ] Give a 1-page "how to use it" sheet for the team
- [ ] Offer the retainer: **Care** as standard, **Growth** for those who want more enquiries
- [ ] Ask for a referral: "Who else do you know who's drowning in admin?"

## Monthly retainer routine

**Care (≈2 hours/month)**
- [ ] Check reviews are still being requested; reply to any new ones
- [ ] Publish 2 Google posts (recent job photos)
- [ ] Website check and any small updates
- [ ] Send the one-page monthly report

**Growth (≈4 hours/month), everything in Care plus:**
- [ ] Refresh ad creative with new job photos
- [ ] Review ad results and adjust
- [ ] 20-minute review call

## Track on every client
Hours spent per step, so the template gets faster and the time estimates in [01-offer.md](01-offer.md) can be corrected.
