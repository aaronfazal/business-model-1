# 01 — The Offer

## The problem we solve

Small carpentry firms are usually great at the work and stretched on everything else:

- Jobs booked across WhatsApp, texts, a paper diary and someone's memory
- Quotes written from scratch in the evenings, sent days late, and lost to faster competitors
- Invoices chased by hand, or not chased at all
- A thin Google profile with a handful of reviews, so new customers pick someone else

## Package 1 — Setup (£1,000 one-off, delivered in 14 days)

| Part | What the client gets |
|---|---|
| **1. Job system** | One job-management tool set up end to end: every job booked into one shared calendar, reusable quote templates using *their* job rates, invoices sent from the job, and automatic payment reminders. Staff added to the app. |
| **2. Google Business Profile** | Full overhaul: services, service area, photos of their work, categories, hours, description. |
| **3. Review engine** | An automatic review request sent to every customer when a job is marked complete. |
| **4. Checkatrade profile** | Tidied and aligned with Google (only if they're already a member — membership is their cost). |
| **5. Website** | **If they have no site, or a poor one:** a one-page site built from our template (services, photos, reviews, quote request form). **If they already have a decent site:** light fixes and the quote form connected to their job system. |

The client pays for their own software subscriptions and any memberships. Our fee covers the setup work.

### Why the website is included rather than a separate core offer

Most carpenters don't want "a website"; they want more jobs and less admin. Selling a website on its own competes with cheap builders. Bundling it into the setup makes the offer more valuable, and because it's built from one template it adds only a few hours. Hosting and updates then become a natural reason to stay on a retainer.

## Founding price (first 2 clients only)

£500 setup, or free setup with a 3-month Care commitment, in exchange for:
- a written or video testimonial
- permission to share before/after numbers (reviews, time spent on admin, average days to get paid)

## The maths to £2,000/month

| Scenario | Monthly income |
|---|---|
| 8 Care clients | £2,000 recurring |
| 3 Growth + 2 Care clients | £2,000 recurring |
| 1 new setup + 4 Care clients | £2,000 (£1,000 one-off + £1,000 recurring) |

Recurring income is the priority: setup fees are lumpy, retainers stack.

## What we don't promise

No guarantees of a number of jobs or revenue, since that depends on their pricing, area and workmanship. We guarantee the **delivery**: the system live in 14 days, or we keep working free until it is.
