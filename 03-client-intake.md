# 03 — Client Intake Form

Sent to every client after they sign. Their answers replace market research: we build from what they tell us.

## The business
1. Business name, trading address, phone, email
2. Number of staff and their roles
3. Areas you cover (towns or postcodes) and how far you'll travel
4. Your main services (e.g. kitchens, fitted wardrobes, doors, decking, staircases, loft conversions)
5. Which jobs do you most want more of? Which would you rather stop doing?

## Your rates
6. Your day rate (per carpenter)
7. Typical price ranges for your 3–5 most common jobs
8. Do you charge a deposit? How much, and when?
9. Payment terms (e.g. due on completion, 7 days, 14 days)

## How you work now
10. How do jobs get booked today (phone, WhatsApp, diary, app)?
11. How do you write and send quotes? Roughly how long does one take?
12. How do you invoice and take payment?
13. How many invoices are overdue right now, roughly?
14. Any software you already use or pay for?

## Getting found
15. Do you have a website? (link)
16. Do you have a Google Business Profile? (link) How many reviews?
17. Are you on Checkatrade or similar? (link)
18. Facebook / Instagram pages (links)
19. Where do most of your jobs come from today?

## For us to set things up
20. 15–30 photos of your best recent work
21. Your logo (if you have one)
22. Access to: Google Business Profile, website, Checkatrade, Facebook page
23. A list of past customers (names + phone/email) we can ask for reviews, if you have one

## Starting point (for your before/after numbers)
24. Hours a week spent on admin (quotes, invoices, chasing)
25. Average days to get paid after finishing a job
26. Number of Google reviews today
